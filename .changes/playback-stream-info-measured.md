The stream info panel now matches the audio panel's glass look and is easier to read: grouped into picture, sound and connection, with plain names (Full HD, H.265, Hardware), a playing/buffering status and an "Explain these numbers" option. It shows the real coded size (anamorphic streams no longer show a wrong one) and the network download speed. Anything not measured is left out.
