Opt-in two-session mode for audio from another channel (off by default, frame-copy engine only, Settings > Playback). Auto Sync is one run per press of Sync: it freezes the side that is ahead once (announced, capped, cache-checked), corrects the rest gently, says "Synced" and then stays silent until pressed again. Manual sync and "Quick sync" are separate; legacy audio-add is unchanged.
